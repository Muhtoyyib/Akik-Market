# Marketing sitemap

Agreed with the client lead on 2026-11-06. A careers page was considered and dropped at their request.

| #   | Page                      | Path                           | Purpose                                                                  |
| --- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Home                      | `/`                            | Brand promise, the three divisions, highlights, proof, social links      |
| 2   | About / Our Story         | `/about`                       | The founder, timeline from phones to engineering to autos, values, team  |
| 3   | Phones & Gadgets          | `/phones-and-gadgets`          | Products, brands, trade-ins, repairs and warranty, store locations       |
| 3   | Engineering & Real Estate | `/engineering`                 | Construction services, houses and land for sale, how buying works        |
| 3   | Autos                     | `/autos`                       | What is sold, financing, stock highlights, how buying works              |
| 4   | Projects / Portfolio      | `/projects`, `/projects/$slug` | Finished buildings, estates and big deliveries                           |
| 5   | Listings preview          | `/listings`                    | Featured properties and vehicles; each opens an enquiry form or WhatsApp |
| 6   | Testimonials              | `/testimonials`                | Customer stories across the three divisions                              |
| 7   | News / Blog               | `/news`, `/news/$slug`         | Stock arrivals, finished projects, announcements                         |
| 8   | Contact                   | `/contact`                     | Enquiry form routed by division, locations, map, WhatsApp and phone      |
| 9   | Legal                     | `/privacy`, `/terms`           | Privacy policy and terms                                                 |

Paths are proposed, not final. The division shops (ordering and payment) are out of scope for this app.
