## Purpose

Every way to reach Akik, by business.

Source: Home's Visit us contact columns.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: One column per business

The section SHALL show one column per business, in growth order (Phones, Engineering, Autos) and in its accent, each with its name, a WhatsApp link, its phone number written locally and dialled internationally, and its hours, as on Home's Visit us.

#### Scenario: Calling Autos

- **WHEN** a visitor taps the Autos number
- **THEN** the phone dials it in international form

### Requirement: Email and social

Under the columns the section SHALL show the email address as a `mailto:` link, and the Instagram and TikTok accounts as text links that open in a new tab with safe link attributes.

#### Scenario: Email link

- **WHEN** a visitor taps the email
- **THEN** their mail app opens addressed to akikmarket@gmail.com
