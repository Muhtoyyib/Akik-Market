## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Share on WhatsApp

The page SHALL end with a closing block whose heading invites customers to share their story, and a `primary` WhatsApp pill to the main number prefilled with "Hello Akik, I'd like to share my story".

#### Scenario: Tapping share

- **WHEN** a visitor taps the pill
- **THEN** WhatsApp opens to the main number with the message ready
