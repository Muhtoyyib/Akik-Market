## Purpose

One customer of this business, in their words.

Source: Matic P6 (testimonial video box), as built for Home's Customer stories, with a single story and no tabs.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: One story

The section SHALL show this business's first story from the shared stories collection in the story box built for Home: photo or video, first name, "customer since" line and quote. There SHALL be no tabs.

#### Scenario: Autos story

- **WHEN** the stories collection has an Autos story
- **THEN** the Autos page shows that story and no tab switch

### Requirement: Placeholders never name real customers

A placeholder story SHALL use a first name only and SHALL NOT show a face.

#### Scenario: Stand-in story

- **WHEN** the story is a placeholder
- **THEN** it has a first name only and a photo with no person in it
