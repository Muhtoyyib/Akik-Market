## Purpose

Other posts to read next.

Source: Matic P9 cards, three.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Three other posts

Under the body, the page SHALL show up to three other posts: the newest about the same business, then the newest of any business to fill the gap, never the current post. With no other posts it SHALL be left out.

#### Scenario: Two posts in total

- **WHEN** the collection has two posts
- **THEN** each post page shows the other one
