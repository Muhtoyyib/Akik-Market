## Purpose

Leading from a post to the business it's about.

Source: Matic P7 banner with an inset link card, as built for Home's Projects banner.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Link to the business

A post about one business SHALL end with a banner whose card names that business and links to its division page once it exists, or opens WhatsApp to that business before then, with the card's heading saying where it goes.

#### Scenario: Car post before the Autos page exists

- **WHEN** a post is about Autos and `/autos` isn't built
- **THEN** the card's heading says it opens WhatsApp and its link opens WhatsApp to the Autos number
