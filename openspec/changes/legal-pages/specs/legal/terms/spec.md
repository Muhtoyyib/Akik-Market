## Purpose

What the terms page must cover once its text is supplied.

Source: the owner's or his lawyer's text; see design D1.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Covers the site, not the shops

The terms SHALL name the legal entity behind the site, say that listings and prices are previews that are confirmed in conversation before any sale, and say that purchases happen in person or in each business's own shop app under that app's terms.

#### Scenario: Listings disclaimer

- **WHEN** the terms render
- **THEN** a section says listed prices and items are confirmed before any sale
