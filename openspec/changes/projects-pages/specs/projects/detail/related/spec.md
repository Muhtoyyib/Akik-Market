## Purpose

Other projects to look at next.

Source: Obvious case-study cards (captured, frame 6), the same card as the index.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Three other projects

Under the gallery, the page SHALL show up to three other projects as index cards: the newest of the same type, then the newest of any type to fill the gap. It SHALL never show the current project. With no other projects it SHALL be left out.

#### Scenario: Only one other project

- **WHEN** the collection has two projects
- **THEN** the related section shows one card
